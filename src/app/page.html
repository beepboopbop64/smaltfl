About this flow

NYC yellow-taxi trips from the TLC's records, one load a day as a feed would bring them, checked, joined to their zones and each borough's hourly weather, all run in this browser. Step through January 2024, or a real incident from the records. Each load is judged against two years of days before it: a dot on a card means news. Tap any card for its story: the news first, then its rows, columns, what it dropped, odd rows and history, each with what you can do about it.
